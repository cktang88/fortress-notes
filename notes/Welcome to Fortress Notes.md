# Welcome to Fortress Notes

Your notes live in a local database on this Mac. Each note is also mirrored to a plain Markdown file in the notes folder, which you can open as an Obsidian vault, so nothing is locked in.

## Try it

- Press **⌘N** for a new note. Its title follows the first line until you give it one.
- Type in **Search notes**: one box finds exact words, meaning and titles together. Press **⌘↩** to ask your notes instead.
- Open the inspector (**⌥⌘I**) to see related notes, backlinks and the text read from pictures and PDFs.
- Press **⌘K** to jump to any note or run any command, and **⇧⌘Space** from any app to capture a thought.
- Mark a **rough** braindump **polished** when it's ready.
- Bring your notebooks over with **File › Import › From OneNote…**.
