# Welcome to Fortress Notes

Your notes live in a local database on this Mac. Each note is also exported, one way, to a plain Markdown file in the notes folder, which you can open as an Obsidian vault, so nothing is locked in.

## Try it

- Press **⌘N** for a new note. Its title follows the first line until you give it one.
- Type in the search field (**Search or ask a question**, top right): one box finds exact words, meaning and titles together.

## Ask your notes

Ask a question in your own words and get a short answer written only from your notes, with every sentence linked to the passage it came from.

- Click **Ask** in the toolbar, next to the search field, or press **⇧⌘A** (View › Ask Your Notes…). Type your question and press **Return**.
- Or type a question straight into the search field, like *When does my passport expire?*, and press **Return**. A question ends in "?" or starts with who, what, when, where, why, how…
- From any search, **⌘↩** asks instead of searching.
- Ask needs an OpenRouter API key: put `OPENROUTER_API_KEY=your-key` in `backend/.env` (it stays on this Mac and is never committed). Everything else works without one.

## More to try

- Open the inspector (**⌥⌘I**) to see related notes, backlinks and the text read from pictures and PDFs.
- Press **⌥⌘F** and type part of a title to jump to any note, and **⇧⌘Space** from any app to capture a thought.
- Bring your notebooks over with **File › Import › From OneNote…**.
